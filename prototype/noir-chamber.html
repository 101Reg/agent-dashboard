WHAT THIS SYSTEM IS ◈

Most people use AI as a chat window — ask a question, get an answer, start over tomorrow. Agent OS is an organization. It has a staff, a memory that never resets, a rulebook written from its own mistakes, and an inspector who audits everything overnight. Hero runs it; it runs the work.

In simple terms

Think of it as a small company where every employee is an AI. There's a chief of staff (the main conversation) that routes work to 8 named specialists. What any of them learns gets written down permanently — 111 memory files so far — so nothing has to be re-explained twice.

When something goes wrong, the mistake doesn't just get fixed — it becomes a rule (22 of them) or an automatic reflex (16 hooks that fire on their own, like catching an unverified number before it reaches Hero). That's why the dashboard's core metric is failure → prevention: the percentage of hits taken that got turned into permanent immunity. Over 100% means the system shipped more armor than it took damage — this week it's at 233%.
